Public suffix data snapshot, vendored at packages/shared/src/public-suffix-rules.json and bundled into the desktop app, extracted on 2026-10-17 from the already installed tldts 7.4.18 data trie. Includes ICANN and PRIVATE rules. Only rule data is vendored; the application does not import or depend on tldts.

Source: https://github.com/remusao/tldts (data derived from https://publicsuffix.org/list/)
